What is Talumi?

Talumi is a free browser game in the io genre. You start as a single grain of dust in an open field of debris. Everything you swallow makes you bigger, and everything bigger than you is trying to swallow you. Grow far enough and your body changes: craters, then molten cracks, then an atmosphere, then rings and moons of your own.

How to play

Move with the mouse or drag anywhere on a touchscreen. Press space, or the on-screen button, to split — splitting is the only way to reach something that is running away, and it leaves your pieces vulnerable for several seconds. Press W to shed mass. Shed mass can be fed to a pulsar until it spawns a second one, which turns the hazard into a weapon against anyone larger than you.

Four modes

Open space puts you on one field with everyone else in the room, with the server deciding what happened rather than any player's browser. A room holds 190 bodies; while fewer people are online, the rest are computer-controlled rivals that say so in their name. Without a connection the same round runs on your own device. Battle royale starts everyone equal and closes the field in stages until one body is left. Clan battle splits a mirrored arena between two clans and scores total mass after five minutes. Friendly match is a mirrored practice arena that pays no rewards, so it cannot be farmed.

Accounts, honour and ranks

An account is free and needed only for online play; with one, your level, Ore and designs live on the server instead of the browser, so they survive a cleared cache. Honour is paid only for swallowing another account in an online round — never for a computer-controlled rival, and the same opponent counts once a day — which is why it cannot be bought or sat out. It carries thirteen ranks, from Cadet to Grand Admiral, shown as a badge beside your name, and twenty-one achievements each pay it once.

What it does not do

No advertising interrupts a round, and nothing sold ever affects play. All 46 designs can be earned — 21 by level, 25 with Ore you win by playing — and the tier of a design is visible on the body itself, so you can tell an experienced player from a merely large one. There are no cookies, no tracking and no third-party content on this page.
