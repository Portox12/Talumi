What is Pulsavi?

Pulsavi is a free browser game in the io genre. You start as a single grain of dust in an open field of stardust. Everything you swallow makes you bigger, and everything bigger than you is trying to swallow you. Grow far enough and your body changes: craters, then molten cracks, then an atmosphere, then rings and moons of your own.

How to play

Move with the mouse or drag anywhere on a touchscreen. Press space, or the on-screen button, to split — splitting is the only way to reach something that is running away, and it leaves your pieces vulnerable for several seconds. Press W to shed mass. Shed mass can be fed to a pulsar until it spawns a second one, which turns the hazard into a weapon against anyone larger than you.

Two game modes

Ascent is the way up: levels, skills, moons and the start bonus count. In the League everyone starts equal — only skill decides. A room holds 190 bodies; while fewer people are online, computer-controlled rivals fill it up, but the player number and the leaderboards count only people. Clans can challenge each other to a clan battle. Without a connection the same round runs on your own device.

Accounts, honour and ranks

An account is free and needed only for online play; with one, your level, Ore and designs live on the server instead of the browser, so they survive a cleared cache. Honour is earned by swallowing another account in an online round — never a computer-controlled rival, and the same opponent counts once a day — from achievements that each pay it once, and a little from the daily bonus; it cannot be bought. It carries twenty ranks, from Cadet to Grand Admiral, shown as a badge beside your name.

What it does not do

No advertising interrupts a round, and in the League nothing you can buy has any effect. Every design can be earned — by level or with Ore won by playing — and the tier of a design is visible on the body itself, so you can tell an experienced player from a merely large one.

Questions

Is Pulsavi free?

Yes. Every design can be earned by playing, and you can play everything without an account; a free account keeps your progress on the server and adds honour, ranks, achievements and clans.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in landscape, with on-screen buttons for splitting and shedding mass. You can add it to your home screen; it then runs full screen.

Who is behind it?

An independent developer in Germany. Questions and feedback: info@pulsavi.io.
