2. Bitte bestätigen

Cancel Pulsavi Premium

Cancel your “Pulsavi Premium” subscription here without signing in. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period; you keep all benefits until then and nothing is charged afterwards. It is faster directly in Google Play: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pulsavi Premium → Cancel subscription.

1. Who is cancelling?

2. Please confirm

You are cancelling the Pulsavi Premium subscription for the account at the earliest possible date. We stop auto-renewal at Google Play and send you a confirmation e-mail right away.

Cancellation received

Please also check Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Questions: info@pulsavi.io.
