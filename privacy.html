3. Storage in your browser

Without an account, the game stores your game progress (level, experience, Ore, best results, unlocked designs, player name and friends list) in your browser's local storage so that it is still there on your next visit. These data do not leave your device and can be removed at any time via your browser settings (“delete website data”). They are strictly necessary for the function you use (section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG).

If the game is added to the home screen as an app, the browser creates a technical cache (service worker cache) with the program files. It contains no personal data and serves only to make the game usable without an internet connection. It can be deleted by removing the app.

If you are signed in, the game stores a random session key in your browser's local storage so that you do not have to enter your password again on your next visit. This is not a cookie, and the key is only sent when the game explicitly includes it. It is strictly necessary for the sign-in you requested and therefore falls under the exception in section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG — no consent is needed for this either. It is deleted when you sign out.

The game also remembers in your browser's local storage the settings you have chosen yourself — volume, music, colour theme, name display and controls. These data do not leave your device, are not analysed and are necessary for the display you requested. You can delete them by removing the website data in your browser.

For the short survey and the rating request (section 6d), the game also remembers in your browser's local storage on how many days you have played, which day was the last one and whether you have already seen the two questions. These data do not leave your device; they only ensure that each question comes at most once.

The site currently does not include any advertising networks or analytics services and does not transmit any data to social networks. It does not load any fonts, scripts, maps or videos from third-party servers.

4. Playing without an account

Without signing in, your player name, game progress, level, currency, daily bonus and friends list stay in your browser's local storage (see section 3) and do not leave your device.

If you play online without an account, section 5 also applies: your player name and your IP address reach the game server. No game progress is stored on the server — except an entry in the title leaderboard (section 6a) if you held the title.

An account is optional. It keeps your progress and is the prerequisite for appearing in the leaderboards — neither would work without a server, because otherwise everyone could write their own values.

  • Password — never in plain text. Only a fingerprint calculated with the scrypt method is stored, from which the password cannot be recovered.
  • Player name and, if you provide it, your country (two letters, for the national ranking and for the age limit for news by e-mail, section 6). The country comes from your entry — when you register, the game suggests it from your browser's language setting, and you can change it —, not from your IP address; no location tracking takes place.

If you are scattered, the game server remembers for 90 seconds, only in its memory, with which mass you can continue playing with a ticket.

The clan also has the balance of its clan bank and the levy the leader sets for it (0 to 5 % of the Ore from each of the members' rounds); all members see both, and the levy is also visible to anyone who wants to join a clan. In addition there are the clan boosts bought from the clan bank (type and duration) and a cash book of the last expenses (date, type, amount – without names).
  • Points in time of the account's creation and last visit and of the last change of player name and country (both can only be changed every 30 days).
  • Sessions — for each signed-in device, a fingerprint of the session key with an expiry date.
  • Month and year of birth — when you register, we ask for your date of birth. We only use the day at that moment to check your age; only the month and year are stored. We do not create an account for anyone under 13; we delete an existing one after your confirmation. Your age is never visible to other players.
  • Acceptance of the terms of use — which version you accepted and when.
  • Language — the game language you last used when registering, signing in or switching in the game, so that messages to you come in this language.
  • Consent to news — if you gave it, the records of it (section 6).
  • IP address during an online round, technically necessary for the connection, and briefly to defend against automated password guessing.

Your friends list stays in your browser and is not stored on the server. For the friends ranking, only the entered names are sent to the server to look up their placings.

What is publicly visible

In the leaderboards and during a round, other players can see your player name, your country, your level, your best results, your Honour with rank and — if you are in a clan — the clan name and tag; a clan's member list shows the name, level, Honour and role of its members. Via “Report this player” in the profile you can report a name to us (section 6c).

Online status: Signed-in players who have your player name in their friends list see “Online” there when you are currently in the game, and “Recently active” if you were there in the last 24 hours – nothing else; never an exact time. The names a player asks about are not stored. You can hide your status at any time in the settings under “Online status”; it is then shown to no one. 6d. Survey and rating request

6f. Push notifications

With an account you can have notifications sent to your device – for example that your daily bonus is ready, that your clan has been challenged or how a season turned out for you. This only happens if you switch it on: in the game via the question after the daily bonus or in the settings under “Notifications”, and then through the permission in your browser. Without switching it on, nothing is set up and nothing is stored. You choose which types you want to receive and can switch off each type and notifications altogether at any time in the settings – or in your browser or operating system.

Which data are processed

When you switch it on, your browser creates a “subscription” with its manufacturer's push service and passes it to the game server. For your account we store: the address of this subscription (a long, random internet address at the push service), two keys with which only your device can decrypt the messages, the types you have chosen, the game language, your device's time zone offset (only the hour difference, no location – so that nothing arrives at night), the time of switching on and of the last delivery, and the number of failed delivery attempts. For the daily bonus reminder we also remember when you last collected it – the reminder comes the next day at the same time, not at night. No IP address, no location, no other device identifier.

We send little: at most one bonus reminder a day and only as long as you have played in the last two weeks; clan messages only if your clan is affected; the season result once per season; the start of happy hour only if you switch on this type specifically; and rarely a message from us, for example about a round that was scored later. We do not send advertising for third parties.

Recipients

Each message goes from the game server to your browser's push service, which forwards it to your device: for Chrome, Samsung Internet and other Chromium browsers Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), for Microsoft Edge Microsoft (Windows Push Notification Services), for Firefox Mozilla (Mozilla Push Service), for Safari, iPhone and iPad Apple (Apple Push Notification service). The content is encrypted according to the web push standard (RFC 8291) so that only your device can read it; the push service sees the address of the subscription, the time and the size of the message, not the text. The browser manufacturers operate these services as part of the browser function that you use with your permission; their servers may be located outside the EU, in particular in the USA. Google and Microsoft are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; for the others, the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or your browser manufacturer's privacy policy, which governs the push service, apply.

Legal basis

Your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR – given by switching it on in the game and the permission in the browser; the question in the game names all types of messages and the off switch beforehand. For the subscription that your browser creates on your device for this, section 25(1) TDDDG. The message about the start of happy hour is a promotion and therefore advertising; it only comes if you switch on this type specifically (section 7(2) no. 2 of the German Act against Unfair Competition, UWG), only during the day in your local time and never to players under 18. Notifications can only be switched on from the age of consent in your country (16 in Germany, Art. 8 GDPR); for this we use the date of birth from your account. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Settings → Notifications → Off, or in your browser's site settings).

Storage period

We delete the subscription as soon as you switch off notifications, sign out on the device or delete your account; likewise as soon as the push service reports that the subscription is no longer valid (for example after the app has been removed or the permission revoked), after ten failed deliveries in a row and at the latest one year after the last delivery.

6i. Chat

With an account you can write in the chat of the map you are playing on (terms of use, section 5a). Without an account there is no chat; it can be switched off completely in the settings.

Which data are processed

The text of your message or the quick phrase chosen, your player name, your account number, in Ascent your clan's tag, the map and the time. These details can be seen by everyone reading the chat on the same map – that is, other players with an account. Their game needs the account number so that they can hide you or report a message from you.

Storage period

Messages are only kept in the game server's memory: the last 50 per map, for at most 30 minutes. They are gone when the server restarts. They are not written to the database or to the server logs.

If someone reports a message, we store it with the reason, the map, the time and the account numbers of the author and the reporter for seven days – so that automatic muting works and we can check an objection. After that the server deletes it automatically. We store a mute with its expiry and level until it has expired and no new one has been added for 30 days.

If you delete your account, we immediately delete your messages in memory, your mute and the reports you have made yourself. Reports about your messages remain until the end of their seven days, because they may be evidence of a possible legal violation.

The server also sends reports with the reason “Threat” as an e-mail via our mailing service Brevo (section 6) to the controller's mailbox – with the message, the player name and the account numbers. We delete this e-mail as soon as the matter has been dealt with, at the latest after twelve months, unless it is needed for criminal proceedings.

How checks are made

The filter runs on our own server; chat messages are not sent to any other service and not to any artificial intelligence. Muting after reports is an automated decision that only concerns the chat and lasts at most seven days. You can object to it (terms of use, section 10); a human will then check it.

Legal basis

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – the chat is part of the game you use with your account. For the filter, reports, mutes and the e-mail for “Threat” additionally Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Articles 16 to 18 of the EU Digital Services Act, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest and that of the other players in a chat without insults, harassment and contact attempts.
