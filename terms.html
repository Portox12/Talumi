4. Fair play

Pulsavi should be fair for everyone. That is why the following is not allowed:

  • cheats, bots, macros and scripts – that is, anything that plays for you or gives you an advantage that others do not have;
  • changing or manipulating the game code, the data transmission to the server or the game's storage, for example to obtain Ore, designs or other values;
  • deliberately exploiting bugs in the game. If you find one, please report it to us via “Report a problem” – thank you!
  • second accounts or arrangements to obtain Honour, ranks, achievements, rewards from “Invite friends” or other advantages for yourself or others – for example by deliberately letting yourself be eaten by your own or an agreed account, or by inviting yourself;

Hunting together, feeding team-mates with ejected mass and discussing tactics in a clan are part of the game and allowed. What is not allowed is deliberately letting yourself be eaten so that someone gets Honour, ranks or rewards – not even within your own clan.

The game has an automatic protection against bots. It can slow down inputs that are too fast or pause a round and ask you for a short check. It does not decide on blocking an account – a human always does that.

5a. Chat

With an account you can write in the chat of the map you are playing on (Ascent or League) – in the game and in the menu. What you write can be seen by everyone who is currently reading the chat on this map. There are no private messages to individual players. Without an account there is no chat. You can switch it off completely in the settings.

The rules

Everything in section 5 applies in the chat, and in addition:

  • no insults, no bullying, no threats and no hate against people or groups;
  • nothing sexual, nothing glorifying violence and nothing that breaks the law;
  • no links, no advertising and no spam (for example the same message over and over);
  • no personal data – neither yours nor anyone else's: no phone numbers, addresses, e-mail addresses and no names on messengers or social networks. Do not ask anyone about their age, where they live, pictures or passwords;
  • do not pretend to be someone else – above all not us as the operator. We never ask you for your password.

Do not give your contact details to anyone from the chat, and do not meet people you only know from here. If someone harasses you, report them and talk to your parents or someone you trust.

How the chat is protected

  • Filter: before a message goes out, the game automatically checks it for banned words, links and contact details. If it gets stuck, it is not sent, and you see the reason straight away. No filter catches everything – that is why there are also the following points.
  • Brake: at most one message every two seconds, and not the same message several times in a row.
  • Report: tap a message, choose “Report” and a reason. You will no longer see the message afterwards.
  • Automatic mute: if at least three different players report messages from the same player within one hour, the game mutes that player automatically – the first time for one hour, the next time within 30 days for 24 hours, after that for seven days each time. Their messages then disappear for everyone; they can still read, just not write. So that reports cannot be misused, only accounts that are at least one day old and have already played some rounds count towards this.
  • Hide: you can hide a player for yourself; this applies on your device, and you can show them again in the settings.

The mute is decided by an automatic system, without a human checking it first. You see straight away until when and why. If you think it is wrong, write to us at info@pulsavi.io: a human will then check the stored reports and lift the mute if it was wrong (section 10). Blocking your account is always decided by a human.

Reporting illegal content and dangers

If you choose the reason “Threat” when reporting, we also receive an e-mail with the message – intended for threats, signs of self-harm and the harassment of children. If there is a danger to life or limb, we inform the police.

You can also report illegal content to us at any time by e-mail to info@pulsavi.io. Please state which message it is about (map, time, player name), why you think it is illegal, your name and e-mail address, and that your information is correct to the best of your knowledge (Article 16 of the EU Digital Services Act).
